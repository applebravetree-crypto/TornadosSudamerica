This map contains every tornado I could find that occurred in the South-American Tornado Corridor, a region much like the United States' Great Plains, extending from central Argentina to southern Brazil & southern Paraguay. Some tornadoes from other areas, such as Chile or the rest of Brazil, are also included, but are not the main focus of this map.

A significant amount of tornadoes from 2010 onwards are sourced from news sites, such as Metsul, g1 globo, Clarín, infobae, etc. Brazilian tornadoes may also be rated by SIMEPAR, a Brazilian meteorological organisation. Tornadoes in Argentina from 2000 onwards are much less well-documented compared to Brazil from 2000 onwards (One notable exception being the Las Flores, Buenos Aires IF3 tornado on 6 May 2026). However, 2 distinct PDF documents exist that document Argentinian tornadoes, one ranging from 1930 - 1988 (María Luisa Altinger de Schwarzkopf, 1988) & one ranging from 1889 - 2017 (Mariano Balbi & Pablo Barbieri, 2017). The María Luisa paper seems to be the source that the Balbi & Barbieri paper get most of their pre-1988 tornadoes from, with one notable exception being the 1889 tornado.
